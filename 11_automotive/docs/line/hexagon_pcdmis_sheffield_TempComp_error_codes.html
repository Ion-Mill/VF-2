This section details the following TempComp error codes:

TempComp-201 Absolute temperature limit exceeded

TempComp-202 Gradient temperature limit exceeded

TempComp-203 Transient temperature limit exceeded

TempComp-204 laser compensation is inactive

TempComp-205 Probe calibration data may be invalid

TempComp-206 Rotary table calibration may be invalid
