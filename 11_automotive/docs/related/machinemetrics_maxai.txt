 Reality on the shop floor disrupts production plans: Machines break down, Setups run long, Materials arrive late, Tribal knowledge overrides system logic, This disconnect is known as the execution gap, and it’s killing our productivity.

 TURN SIGNALS INTO UNDERSTANDING 
 From cycle times to labor tickets and setup notes, every data point is mapped to a common model. This standardization gives Max AI the clarity to understand what's happening - and why it matters. 
 UNIFIED DATA MODEL 
 Every job, machine, and event is automatically mapped into a connected graph of your operation, so Max can follow the relationships between them instead of querying one table at a time. 
 REAL-TIME CONTEXT 
 Max AI enriches raw data with operational meaning - connecting alarms to active jobs, setups to specific parts, and delays to real delivery risk. 
 EVENT-DRIVEN ARCHITECTURE 
 Max AI processes production data as discrete events, enabling real-time, context-aware automation without polling or delay.
