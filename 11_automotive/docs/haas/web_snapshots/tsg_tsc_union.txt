Introduction
This troubleshooting guide applies to the 30T, 40T, and 50T compensating unions on TSC 300/1K. 
For other TSC issues, refer to 
TSC-300/1K - Troubleshooting Guide - CHC
 or 
TSC-300/1K - Troubleshooting Guide - NGC
.
 Note:
 There are several versions of the TSC compensating coolant union in the field. If you are unsure which compensating TSC union is installed on the machine, refer to the 
Compensating TSC Union - Identification Guide
.
TSC Union Troubleshooting
The following service video shows how to troubleshoot a TSC Compensating Union.
NOTE:
 This video is for reference only.
Compensating TSC Union Troubleshooting
Symptom
Possible Cause
Corrective Action
A large flow of coolant comes out of the drain when the TSC is turned on for the first time.
The union piston is not seated.
Turn on TSC again with a TSC tool in the spindle and then quickly turn it off. The back pressure, or the TSC Purge cycle will likely push the piston down. Or, disconnect the hydraulic hose from the top of the union and use a screwdriver to manually seat the piston.
The amount of coolant flow from the drain tube is more than a few drops.
Runout on the carbide tip or damage to the piston cup seal.
Check the motor shaft for runout. Inspect the piston cup seal.
The union is leaking coolant.
Leaky drain tube or no air supply to the union.
The drain tube connection is not watertight and requires glue to seal it during union assembly, make sure the hose clamp is tight. Check the solenoid and hose for air supply problems.
Improper draining from coolant union.
Swap out vaccuum generator assembly with coolant purge body assembly. Order kit 
93-4852 TSC CLNT PURGE ASSY W/MACH BODY SVC KIT
.
The union makes a high pitch ringing noise when running TSC that does not go away after a few minutes of running.
TSC union alignment, or shaft runout.
Check the motor shaft for runout. Inspect the piston cup seal. Re-align the union with no hoses connected.
The union makes a high pitched ringing noise when running TAB that does not go away after a few minutes of running.
Excessive back pressure.
Try using a tool with larger orifices to reduce back pressure or try turning on TSC briefly to lubricate the seals.
Major leaking from the side of the union.
Coolant buildup during tool changes.
Replace coolant union. Order kit 
93-4852 TSC CLNT PURGE ASSY W/MACH BODY SVC KIT
. Reconfigure hose (see section below).
Noise from drain tube.
Pressure in drain tube connection. 
Replace coolant union. Order kit 
93-4852 TSC CLNT PURGE ASSY W/MACH BODY SVC KIT
. Reconfigure hose (see section below).
Reconfigure Hose
If the TSC is orientated with the elbow fitting side connected to the TSC Union [1] re-orient the hose so that the non-elbow side is connected to the TSC Union [2].
