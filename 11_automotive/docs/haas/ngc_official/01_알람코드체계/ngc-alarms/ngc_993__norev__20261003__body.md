## Alarm Search Results:**993****AMPLIFIER SHORT CIRCUIT**

---

An amplifier short has been reported on the specified axis. This can be caused by any of the motor leads being shorted together or shorted to the 320V return. – A preceding alarm will indicate the axis which has a short circuit.
