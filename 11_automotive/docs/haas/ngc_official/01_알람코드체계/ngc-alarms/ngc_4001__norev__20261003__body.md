## Alarm Search Results:**4001****LOW AIR DURING TOOL CHANGE**

---

Pressto play video related to this content. There was not enough air to keep the tool unclamped during a tool change. Once the air pressure has been restored, use RECOVERY to correct the tool changer. Ensure sufficient air volume is available: Actuate the air gun while doing a tool change If the pressure drops more then 10 psi you do not have sufficient air flow Take a look at your incoming line Lines should be 3/8 of an inch minimum 1/2 inch is the recommended size Does the line have unnecessary bends or fittings Check your shop compressor Is it large enough for all the machines connected to it
