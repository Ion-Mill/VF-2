## Alarm Search Results:**991****AMPLIFIER OVER TEMPERATURE**

---

An Amplifier's temperature is above it's limit. – A preceding alarm will indicate which amplifier is above it's temperature limit. This can be caused by high axis load for an extended period of time. High shop temperature can cause this alarm to occur more often. Allow time for the amplifier to cool. Adjust your program to reduce axis loads. Clear all alarms.
