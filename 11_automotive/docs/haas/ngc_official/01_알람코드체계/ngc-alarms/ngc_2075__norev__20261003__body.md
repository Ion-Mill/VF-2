## Alarm Search Results:**2075****AXIS LUBRICATION RESERVOIR EMPTY**

---

The lubrication pressure dropped faster than expected. Refill the lubricant reservoir. If the reservoir has recently been refilled this alarm may appear for several lubrication cycles until the air has been purged from the system.
