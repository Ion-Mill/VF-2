## Alarm Search Results:**994****AMPLIFIER OVERLOAD**

---

The Amplifier has sensed a high load for an extended period of time. This can be caused by running the amplifier at high loads for an excessive amount of time. – A preceding alarm will indicate which axis has overloaded. Check that your program has the correct speeds and feeds for the material and the cutting tool. Make sure the correct tool for the application is being used. Check for dull or broken tools.
