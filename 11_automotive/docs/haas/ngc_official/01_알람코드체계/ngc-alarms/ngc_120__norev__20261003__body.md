## Alarm Search Results:**120****LOW AIR PRESSURE OR FLOW**

---

The compressed air supplied to the machine is below the minimum pressure or flow required. Check the volume and flow capacity of your air source. You may have enough pressure but not enough flow to complete a tool change or other air driven function.
