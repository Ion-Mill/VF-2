Service Home 992 AMPLIFIER OVER CURRENT
Recently Updated Last updated: 02/27/2026

Alarm Search Results: 992 AMPLIFIER OVER CURRENT

An Amplifier has sensed excessive current going to the motor. – A preceding axis alarm will indicate which axis amplifier had the fault. This can be caused by a stalled motor, a high motor load for a short duration, Entering a heavy cut with a dull or broken tool can cause this alarm. Check your tools. Check your speeds and feeds.
