    "We design and build custom measurement and custom quality solutions for manufacturers. Our customers come to us for custom designs. We design a solution for them, detail it out, then the parts will come out here to the machine shop where we make the parts and assemble things into a turnkey solution. The VF-2 was a great fit for us. It allows us very easy setup and intergrates very well with our designs. We don’t have to wait 2-3 weeks for the print to go to an outside job shop to negotiate the PO. We can have the need for a part and have it made in the same day."
