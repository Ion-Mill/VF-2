=== Problem -> Cause -> Solution (excerpt of rendered table) ===

[Vibration]
- Weak fixture -> support against cutting-force direction; reduce ap; coarse/differential pitch, more positive cutter; small corner radius & parallel land; fine-grain uncoated/thin-coated insert.
- Axially weak workpiece -> 90 deg square-shoulder positive cutter; lower axial force (lower ap, smaller radius); check tool wear and holder run-out; improve tool clamping.
- Too long tool overhang -> minimize overhang; coarse differential pitch; increase fz; reduce ap; up milling in finishing; for solid carbide end mills try fewer teeth and/or higher helix.
- Irregular table feed -> try up milling; tighten feed mechanism.
- Cutting data -> reduce vc, increase fz, change ap.
- Vibration in corners -> program larger corner radii with reduced feed.

[Chip jamming] (long-chipping materials, full slotting)
- Improve chip evacuation (rich, well-directed coolant or compressed air); reduce fz; split deep cuts; up milling in deep slots; coarse pitch; solid carbide end mills with 2-3 edges and/or higher helix.

[Re-cutting of chips] (slotting/pocketing, deep cavities on vertical machines)
- Evacuate chips by air/coolant (prefer internal coolant); change tool path; reduce fz; split deep cuts.

[Unsatisfactory surface finish]
- Excessive feed per rev -> check axial insert setting, spindle run-out; fn <= 70% of parallel-land width; wiper inserts for finishing.
- Vibration -> see Vibration.
- Built-up edge formation -> increase vc to raise temperature; turn off cutting fluid; sharp edge with smooth rake; positive geometry; cermet grade.
- Back-cutting -> check spindle tilt (~0.10 mm/1000 mm); spindle axial run-out (TIR) <= 7 um in finishing; reduce radial force; smaller cutter.
- Workpiece frittering -> decrease fz; closer pitch; reposition cutter for thinner exit chip; 45 deg entering angle; sharp insert; monitor flank wear.

[Burr formation]
- (HRSA/stainless, notch wear) large radius, low entry angle, ap = 0.5 x radius.

[Insert wear section - relevant items]
- Flank wear -> reduce vc / increase fz / more wear-resistant grade; uneven wear from run-out: keep run-out < 0.02 mm, check chuck/collet, minimize protrusion, higher helix (>=45 deg) for solid end mills.
- Chipping -> tougher grade, stronger edge; may be caused by built-up edge.
- Built-up edge (BUE): cutting zone temperature too low; very sticky materials such as low-carbon steel, stainless and ALUMINIUM -> increase vc, increase fz, positive geometry, use oil mist or cutting fluid. BUE causes poor surface finish and edge frittering when torn away.
- Thermal cracks -> coolant copiously or not at all.
