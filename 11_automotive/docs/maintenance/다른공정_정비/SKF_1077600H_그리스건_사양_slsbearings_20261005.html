Details

The SKF Grease Guns are ideal for agricultural, industrial and construction industries and for private use. The SKF Grease Guns are delivered with a 300 mm (12 in.) high pressure hose with a hydraulic gripping nozzle


Features:

  • For use with cartridges and loose grease
  • Knurled body for firm and safe grip
  • High quality steel is dent-resistant for easy cartridge loading
  • Special piston design for smooth emptying of cartridges


Specifications:

  • Drive: Manual
  • Maximum pressure: 400 bar
  • Volume per stroke: Approx 1,5 cm3 (0.09 in. 3)
  • Reservoir: Loose grease (ca. 500 cm3) or grease cartridges
