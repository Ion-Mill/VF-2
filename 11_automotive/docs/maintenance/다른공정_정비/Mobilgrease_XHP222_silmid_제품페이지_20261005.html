Product Information

Designed to outperform conventional products by applying cutting edge, proprietary, lithium complex manufacturing technology. Formulated to provide excellent high temperature performance with superb adhesion, structural stability and resistance to water contamination. High level of chemical stability and excellent protection against rust and corrosion.

Features:

  • Superb resistance to water washout and spray-off
  • Highly adhesive and cohesive structure
  • Excellent rust and corrosion resistance
  • Very good resistance to thermal, oxidative and structural degradation at high temperature
  • Very good anti-wear and EP performance
  • Broad multi-purpose application
