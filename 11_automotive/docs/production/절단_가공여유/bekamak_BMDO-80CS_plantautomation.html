Profile

Circular saw machine BMDO-80CS

Performance
 

  • Vertical Slide for maximum cutting power and rigidity
  • Standard with Speed Inverter for speeds of 20-150 m/min.
  • Electric Servo Feeding for +/- 0.05 ~ 0.1mm Cutting Length precision (per stroke)
  • Capacity up to 80mm in Tube and Solid Bars
  • Superior Gear Box design for maximum machine life and performance
  • Flexible Gripper Feeding + Floating Carriage allow for maximum feeding precision even in material which is not perfectly straight
  • Cutting Examples
    • D. 50mm solid bar, L = 100mm, cycle time is 11sec
    • O.D. 75mm solid bar, L = 50mm, cycle time is 26sec


Software
 

  • Intuitive and User Friendly Interface with Touch Screen system
  • Safety Covers for maximum operator protection and comfort
  • 100 programs capacity with 50 steps each


Head for ferrous material not with endless screw, but with cemented and ground helical gear for obtaining a high operating rate without any play or vibration of the saw-blade.

Head with inverter allowing a continuous change of the cutting speed.
