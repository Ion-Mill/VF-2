Quick User Guide
Gabby

Hi, I’m Gabby, Tungaloy’s cutting tool assistant. 👋

I can help you with:

  • Tool recommendations for Turning, Grooving/Parting, Milling, and Holemaking
  • Catalog checks for item numbers, dimensions, compatibility, grades, and series
  • Tungaloy alternatives for competitor products
  • Follow-up changes like “same conditions, but SUS304” or “change the diameter to 80 mm”

To start, tell me what you want to machine and the key conditions you know.

Examples:

  • “External turning, S45C, work dia. 60 mm, roughing.”
  • “Shoulder milling, tool dia. 80 mm, ap 3 mm, ae 40 mm.”
  • “Drilling, hole dia. 12 mm, depth 30 mm, material SUS304.”
  • “Find a Tungaloy alternative for a competitor cutter.”

Useful details: material, operation type, diameter, depth/width, metric or inch, machine limits, coolant, and whether you prefer productivity, stability, or tool life.
