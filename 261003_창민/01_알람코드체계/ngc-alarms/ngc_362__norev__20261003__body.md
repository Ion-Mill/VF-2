## Alarm Search Results:**362****TOOL-LIFE LIMIT REACHED**

---

A tool has expired and there are no additional tools to replace it. Inspect the expired tool, and replace it if necessary. Update the tool offset. Reset the tool life in the Advanced Tool Management (ATM) tab. The tab is under the CURRENT COMMANDS tab. Highlight the usage counter for the expire tool, press [0] (zero), and then press [ENTER].
