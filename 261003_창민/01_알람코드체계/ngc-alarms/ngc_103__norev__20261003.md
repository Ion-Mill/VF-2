## Alarm Search Results:**103****AXIS SERVO ERROR TOO LARGE**

---

The axis cannot move to the commanded position and the error grew too large. Clean chips from the machine. Check your program for errors and correct speeds and feeds. Check for chips on tools and tool-holders. Check for dull tools. Check for a machine crash.
