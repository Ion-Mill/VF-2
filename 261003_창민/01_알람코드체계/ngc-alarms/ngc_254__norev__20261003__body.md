## Alarm Search Results:**254****SPINDLE MOTOR OVERHEAT**

---

The Spindle motor has overheated. This can be caused by running the spindle at higher than 100% load for extended periods or by starting and stopping the spindle motor multiple times, for short durations, while using high speeds. Adjust your program to lower the average load or duty cycle where possible. Check for dull or damaged tools.
